# Changes

PROVIDES the Change coordination contract for defining, maturing, persisting, authoring, and auditing one intended Output
SO THAT product engineers and agent sessions coordinating work
CAN move a prioritized Output from proposal to executable work without placing mutable work state in product truth

## Assertions

- A Change record has YAML front matter with the closed key set `title`, `product`, `maturity`, `lifecycle`, `predecessors`, `blocked_by`, and `parent`, plus a body that opens with `## Intent` and adds the sections each Maturity requires in the final format; every key is required, and an unknown key is a defect ([audit:record-front-matter]).
- `change-standards` carries one independently loadable Definition of Ready for each Maturity level: Proposed, Framed, Sliced, and Executable ([audit:dor-per-maturity]).
- No Definition of Ready requires an operator review statement at Proposed, and each Definition of Ready is the complete criterion set for its Maturity ([audit:dor-no-review-statement]).
- `change-standards` holds each Definition of Ready to what review needs: Proposed requires the Intent's What, Why, and Evidence, the exact text of every decision the Output adds or amends, and every record and Node the Output touches; Framed adds a Spec mutations block stating each affected or intended Node's assertions and leaves no question open ([audit:review-bar]).
- `change-standards` places `## Assumptions` between `## Spec mutations` and `## Slice`, holding each question the Frame settled as its answer and one sentence of reason ([audit:assumptions-section]).
- The change audit, run by `audit-change` through its `change-auditor` wrapper, raises a blocking finding only for a failed Definition of Ready criterion of the declared Maturity, and returns every other finding `filed` at a non-blocking severity ([audit:final-format-sections]).
- `change-standards` selects the 4.0 Change chapter — `versions/4.0/methodology/change/changes.md` inside the declared `methodology.source` — for a `methodology.version` declaration of `4.0` or `4.0.N` with `N` a non-negative integer, states that comparison, and rejects every other declaration ([audit:methodology-chapter-selection]).
- `author-change` runs one workflow per Maturity level; each workflow loads only that level's Definition of Ready and advances Maturity only when the Definition of Ready holds and the store shows the Product's Maintainer's move of the Change out of `Submitted` at the Maturity it leaves, which is the authority for that Maturity ([audit:maturity-workflows]).
- `author-change`, `audit-change`, and `change-standards` read the authority for leaving a Maturity from the Product's Maintainer's Lifecycle move out of `Submitted` alone, with its actor and time; `confirm-change` posts no comment; and `confirmation-record` and every `Rejection:` path leave `lifecycle.md`, `confirm-change`, and `author-change` ([audit:authority-move]).
- `change-standards`, `author-change`, and `release-change` each state one order for a rejection at Framed or Sliced — the holder's release first, the rejecting Maturity lowering of one level second (not written by `author-change`), the rejecting move out of `Submitted` third, then further refinement only after a session claims the Change again — and state that a rejection at Proposed leaves the Change `Submitted` ([audit:rejection-lowering]).
- The change audit raises a finding on a record that carries execution status — an `## Artifacts` section, a link to produced content, a Node's state, or a record of progress — blocking where the declared Maturity's Definition of Ready names the rule and `filed` otherwise ([audit:no-execution-status]).
- Change authoring at Executable writes no result or Verifier into the record, and execution derives them from the merge composition ([audit:executable-derivation-at-execution]).
- ALWAYS: `author-change` repairs a valid change-audit finding across every instance the cited criterion covers and nothing the criterion does not require, reading the finding's examples as instances, checks every same-class site and every section a repair changes before it dispatches a new change audit, and reports for each repair the criterion it applies and the check that shows the class closed ([audit])
- ALWAYS: before `author-change` dispatches the first change audit on a Leaf candidate at Framed, Sliced, or Executable, it traces each result the Intent's Evidence promises to the Spec mutations assertion or the Governance mutations decision that carries it, either destination satisfying the trace; it names each result that reaches neither, repairs the candidate, and sweeps it again before the dispatch, and the trace never enters the record ([audit:promised-result-trace])
- `author-change` dispatches the `change-auditor` at most once per revision, publishes whatever the audit finds after repairing what takes a minute, and records a blocking finding by publishing at the highest Maturity whose blocking criteria the record meets ([audit:one-audit-per-revision]).
- The Framed workflow of `author-change` settles every open question with a justified assumption, its answer and one sentence of reason, and sends a question no assumption can defend, with its best answer, to the superior that assigned the refinement, the agent that gave the task or the operator when no agent did, while it frames every part that does not depend on the answer ([audit:justified-assumptions]).
- `audit-change` reads the complete record front matter first, judges one record against the Definition of Ready for its declared Maturity, and emits a structured Agentic verdict under [`spx/31-outcomeeng.enabler/31-verification.enabler/14-verification.pdr.md`](spx/31-outcomeeng.enabler/31-verification.enabler/14-verification.pdr.md), whose result carries the run token, the rendered projection's run-level fields, every finding payload verbatim, and the one command that reproduces the complete rendered projection from the sealed run ([audit:audit-verdict-contract]).
- ALWAYS: `audit-change` treats a new record as touched text in full, and a revision as touched text in each revised section and each section the revision invalidates ([audit])
- ALWAYS: `audit-change` reaches the candidate, every repository path, and the SPX store only through its bundled Python runner, and never invokes `rm`, `mktemp`, a shell redirect to a file, or one of its scripts other than as the runner invocation the skill prescribes ([audit:runner-only-access]).
- ALWAYS: `audit-change` issues each runner invocation as its own command, never chained with `&&` or `;` and never piped into another command, and a nonzero runner exit stops the audit with a blocked result naming the request and the exit status ([audit:one-command-per-invocation]).
- NEVER: `audit-change` or its `change-auditor` wrapper writes a file; every payload passes over stdin and stdout, and the SPX run journal holds the run ([audit:auditor-writes-no-file]).
- Persistence writes each front-matter field to its one home in the configured store and reads each back unchanged; the store body carries the Intent and the sections its Maturity adds, without front matter, no field is written to a second home, a store limit never shapes the record, and the persistence skill instruction selects the client for the configured store ([audit:persistence-field-homes]).
- A record whose front matter does not carry the contract's closed key set is outside the contract; it receives no migration, alias, inferred front matter, body-line lineage interpretation, or audit verdict, and the Auditor reports it as outside the contract ([audit:outside-contract-record]).
- `claim-change` claims only an `Available` record whose Product is the configured Product, or a root that carries no Product: it adds the holder, records the Claim naming the claiming session and the worktree root the Change is claimed for — the session's own assigned root, or a root it names — and moves Lifecycle `Available` to `Claimed`, writing neither Maturity nor any body section; any other Lifecycle, an existing holder, or a field mismatch is reported without mutation ([audit:claim-change-contract]).
- Claiming a Change returns the newest Handoff's `Branch or PR` line and continues execution from the Handoff's next block, or from the first block when no Handoff exists ([audit:claim-resume]).
- Releasing a Change writes the Handoff into the Change — branch or changeset with its head commit, the completed Governance mutations and Spec mutations blocks and the next one, blockers, and hazards, never a secret — removes the holder, moves Lifecycle `Claimed` to `Available`, or to `Submitted` when the release submits the Change, and leaves Maturity unchanged, so any agent session may claim an `Available` Change next; it runs only for a session whose assigned worktree root equals the winning Claim's, and reports any other caller without mutation ([audit:release-contract]).
- Closing a Change takes one terminal Lifecycle value, refuses any value outside `Applied` and `Abandoned`, refuses `Applied` for a Leaf while the merge commit of the pull request or branch its newest Handoff names is not an ancestor of the default branch, a Node lacks a result the merge composition for its malleability after selects, an amended or new record lacks an approving decision-record audit, or the Intent's Evidence is unmet, refuses `Applied` for a Parent while a child is open or its last child closed other than `Applied`, closes a root only on the operator's direction, and moves `Claimed` to the named value after writing the terminal record; it runs only for a session whose assigned worktree root equals the winning Claim's, and reports any other caller without mutation ([audit:close-contract]).
- `close-change` judges each Node of the Spec mutations from its status claim when the Node's directory on the default branch holds `spx.status.json`; for a Node whose directory holds none, it takes the Node to hold every result its merge composition selects when the newest Handoff's `Completed blocks` line lists the Node's block and every required check the merge transport reports for the merged head of the pull request that Handoff names concluded success, and it refuses `Applied` otherwise, a Handoff that names a pushed branch rather than a pull request included ([audit:close-results-without-claim]).
- The plugin ships no `/pickup`, `/handoff`, or `/issue` skill and no session-queue entry path; a follow-up is a Proposed Change created through `author-change` ([audit:no-pickup-handoff-issue]).
- Change authoring writes each record from its Maturity's template in the final format, one template per Maturity, and no template carries `## Nodes`, `## Assertion operations`, `## Decisions`, `## Mutations`, or `## Activities` ([audit:maturity-templates]).
- The change audit counts the title's and the Intent's words deterministically and reports a title over 15 words or an Intent over 60 words as a non-blocking finding naming the section, the limit, and the count ([audit:intent-word-limit]).
- Change authoring splits a Sliced Change by creating children that name it in `parent` and removing its own Governance mutations and Spec mutations, merges two Changes by closing the absorbed one `Abandoned` and adding it to the survivor's `predecessors`, and reframes a Change in place, lowering its Maturity to `Proposed` ([audit:split-merge-reframe]).
- The change audit rejects a record that carries a Governance mutations or Spec mutations block while another Change names it in `parent`, and a Parent at Executable ([audit:parent-child-rejections]).
- After `close-change` closes a Change `Applied` whose Parent has a Parent and no open child, the same session claims that Parent through `claim-change` and closes it `Applied` through `close-change`, repeating upward; it never closes a root this way, and a Parent whose last child closed `Abandoned` stays open ([audit:parent-cascade-close]).
- Closing a Change establishes that its changeset is integrated by `git merge-base --is-ancestor` of the merge commit of the pull request or branch the newest Handoff names and the default branch exiting zero ([audit:changeset-integration-check]).
- `report-changes` lists each open Change of the declared store that passes its filters — Product, Maturity, Lifecycle, holder, and blocked state, each optional — as one row carrying the Change's number, URL, title, Product, Maturity, Lifecycle, Priority, holder, and Parent exactly as the store holds them, orders the rows by the Priority order the coordination overlay declares, a Change with no Priority standing with the last value, and then by ascending issue number, and reads every page of the store, so the rows equal the matching open Changes whatever the number of pages ([test](tests/test_report_changes.property.l1.py))
- A row's root maps to the Change reached by following `Parent` numbers to a Change with none, and to `unresolved` when the chain closes on a cycle or an absent Change; its blockers map to the Change's native dependencies, each with its state; its changeset maps the `Branch or PR` line of the newest `Handoff:` comment to the named branch or pull request with its head commit, adding the pull request's checks when the line names a pull request, and to `none` when the Change has no Handoff or the line reads `none`; and its next action maps to that Handoff's `Next block` line verbatim, and to `none recorded` when the Change has no Handoff ([test](tests/test_report_changes.mapping.l1.py))
- A row's waiting state maps a `Submitted` Change to `confirmation: <Maturity>`, a `Claimed` Change to `holder: <login>` with the session and claim root of its winning Claim, an `Available` Change with an open blocker to `blockers: <numbers>`, and any other `Available` Change to `claim`, and adds `checks` to a `Claimed` Change whose pull request holds a check that has not concluded success ([test](tests/test_report_changes.mapping.l1.py))
- NEVER: `report-changes` returns a shorter list in place of a failure; a read that fails, returns unreadable output, or fills a bound — `100 issues per page, 10 pages`, `50 field values`, `100 blockers, one page`, `100 assignees`, or `100 events per page, 10 pages` — yields a blocked result naming the failed read or the bound and no row ([test](tests/test_report_changes.compliance.l1.py))
- NEVER: `report-changes` writes; it runs reading `gh` commands only, posts no comment, writes no field, assignee, or blocker, and its script writes no file ([test](tests/test_report_changes.compliance.l1.py))
- ALWAYS: `report-changes` reads the Change store and the Priority order from `spx/local/coordination.md` and passes them to its bundled script as request values, and the script reads no overlay and no repository content ([audit:report-request-values])

### Mapping

- ALWAYS: `read-relations` maps a `Parent` number field value, integer or float, to the issue number it names and an absent value to `null` ([test](tests/test_audit_change_relations_parent.mapping.l1.py))

### Compliance

- ALWAYS: `read-relations` returns `absorbers`, the numbers of the issues whose `Predecessors` field value, split at every comma followed by one space, names the named issue, and never the issues that the named issue's own `Predecessors` value names ([test](tests/test_audit_change_relations.compliance.l1.py))
- ALWAYS: `read-relations` reads the store's issue pages in order until the store reports no further page, so a child on any page is returned, and returns a blocked result naming `100 issues per page, 10 pages` when the tenth page still reports a further page ([test](tests/test_audit_change_relations.compliance.l1.py))
- ALWAYS: `read-relations` returns a blocked result naming `50 issue field values per issue` when an issue's field-value connection reports a further page, and returns no Product or Parent read from that partial connection ([test](tests/test_audit_change_relations.compliance.l1.py))
- NEVER: the bundled Python runner of `audit-change` writes a file; each invocation reads one JSON request on stdin and writes one JSON result on stdout, and the SPX run journal is the only state that persists between requests ([test](tests/test_audit_change_run.compliance.l1.py))
- `confirm-change` moves a `Submitted` Change to `Available` and posts no comment; it writes no Maturity and no body section, and reports any other Lifecycle without mutation ([audit]).
- `audit-change` reads every authority event from the store's field-change events, never from a comment or the body, and judges a record carrying body authority text as defective ([audit]).
- The `audit-change` runner's `read-authority` operation reads a Change's field-change events at 100 per page and at most 10 pages, reads no comment, and returns a blocked result naming the bound when the read fills it ([test](tests/test_audit_change_authority.compliance.l1.py)).
- ALWAYS: `audit-change` judges a revision of a published record only on the sections the revision changes and on unchanged text the revision makes false, reading the published body from the store; a finding on any other section is returned `filed`, and a record with no published body is judged whole ([audit])
- ALWAYS: `author-change` dispatches the `change-auditor` for a revision of a stored Change with the candidate path and the canonical `<owner>/<repo>#<N>` store reference in the task message, and the `change-auditor` sets the `audit-change` request's `issue` from that reference and from nothing else ([audit])
- ALWAYS: the `read-published` operation of the `audit-change` runner takes exactly an operation name and a canonical `<owner>/<repo>#<N>` issue identity, reads that issue's body from the store through one `gh` query, writes nothing, and returns the body, the empty string when the store holds none; a malformed request is `invalid-request`, a failed or unrunnable `gh` call is `command-failed`, and a store answer without a body string is `unreadable-output` ([test](tests/test_audit_change_published.compliance.l1.py))
