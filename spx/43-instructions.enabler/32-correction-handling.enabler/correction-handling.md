# Correction Handling

PROVIDES correction of current behavior and durable repair of the instruction that failed
SO THAT sessions receiving operator feedback
CAN prevent recurrence while preserving ongoing authorized work

The `incorporate-correction` skill composes the owning skill or subagent authoring workflow. It carries incident diagnosis and consequence repair without taking over another workflow's artifacts, authority, or publication procedure.

## Assertions

- Every operator correction to behavior, reasoning, workflow, or instructions changes current work immediately within its stated scope; independent authorized work continues while durable repair proceeds.
- A reusable correction produces a concrete repair to the owning instruction in the same turn. A factual correction local to an artifact or an explicit one-time exception receives a supported local disposition without becoming a general rule.
- Diagnosis distinguishes an absent instruction from activation, loading, stale distribution, conflicting authority, ambiguity, and failure to execute a loaded rule. Finding an existing rule or lacking a loading receipt alone never settles the cause; unsupported causes remain explicitly uncertain.
- Consequence tracing follows evidenced dependencies through earlier decisions and work, including completed and cross-product results. Each affected item receives a supported disposition through its owner; repairing an instruction alone never establishes that its consequences are repaired.
- Durable repairs target authored source through its owning workflow. Existing authorization remains effective within its scope; a correction grants no general authority over home configuration, generated caches, another holder's work, or a completed integration.
- Verification exercises the original failure and an adjacent case whose valid behavior must remain intact. Reports distinguish prepared, verified, published, and actively loaded states with separate evidence, preserving actionable continuation in the owning Change when work remains.
- A correction about process discussion displacing delivery causes the next authorized artifact-producing action to complete alongside the instruction repair; diagnosis and another plan alone do not satisfy it.
